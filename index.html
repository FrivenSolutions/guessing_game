Tilt Guess

Pick a category. Then hold the phone on your forehead, screen out.

How to play
  1. Pick a category.
  2. Hold the phone sideways on your forehead. The screen faces your friends.
  3. Your friends describe the word on the screen.
  4. Guessed it? Nod forward (screen tips toward the floor).
  5. Want to skip? Lean back (screen tips toward the ceiling).
  6. Bring the phone back upright before the next word.

No tilt sensor? Use the keyboard: Down arrow = correct, Up arrow = skip.

Place on forehead

Hold the phone upright. The game starts by itself.
